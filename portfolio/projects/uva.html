Overview

University Digital Transformation Outcomes

Architected comprehensive digital transformation for University of Virginia integrating learning management, alumni networks, and administrative systems.

Developed unified platform supporting 25,000+ students and faculty.

Implemented advanced analytics for student success and institutional effectiveness.

The transformation created a seamless digital ecosystem connecting academic, administrative, and alumni communities.

Advanced features include personalized learning paths, predictive analytics for student retention, and comprehensive research collaboration tools.

Led platform strategy, UX/UI design, and delivery for a large-scale digital storytelling and education initiative documenting the integration of Black students and teachers into formerly segregated classrooms.

Designed and built a living documentary platform combining archival media, first-hand narratives, and structured content systems to support education, research, and public access.

Defined content architecture, metadata models, and governance workflows to ensure historical accuracy, long-term preservation, and academic usability.

Collaborated with educators, historians, and institutional stakeholders across the University of Virginia and partner programs to translate complex civil rights history into an accessible, scalable digital experience.

Increased alumni engagement by 50% with new networking and lifelong learning platforms.

Platform recognized as national leader in higher education technology.

Generated significant research funding through improved collaboration tools.

Established UVA as model for digital transformation in prestigious universities.
