Overview

Founded and led PICEUS as a product-driven AI platform focused on building governed, multimodal, and agentic intelligence systems for regulated and high-risk environments. Architected a unified orchestration framework enabling poly-agent coordination, multimodal data pipelines, and adaptive reasoning systems within zero-trust, policy-driven constraints. Integrated AI governance, identity, security, and tokenized incentive systems into a cohesive platform designed for auditability, controlled autonomy, and enterprise deployment. Operated across the full lifecycle from vision through execution, aligning AI architecture, product design, compliance, and operational integration to move AI systems from experimentation into secure, deployable infrastructure.

Outcomes

Key Results and Achievements

Delivered a unified AI platform that enabled coordinated multi-agent systems, real-time compliance monitoring, and governed decision-making across enterprise environments. Reduced regulatory and operational friction through automated governance, policy enforcement, and audit-ready system design while improving system reliability and scalability. Established secure identity, payment, and tokenization frameworks supporting trusted interactions and incentive models at scale. Enabled adaptive learning and continuous improvement through vector and graph-based reasoning systems, resulting in durable, production-ready AI infrastructure capable of operating safely and predictably in complex, regulated ecosystems.
