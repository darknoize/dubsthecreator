Overview

Healthcare Platform Architecture and Implementation

Led product, UX/UI, and platform design for the next-generation WebMD experience, taking over design and delivery from an external agency and transitioning the platform to an in-house, product-driven model.

Directed the redesign of core platform architecture, navigation, and content systems across web, mobile, applications, video, and digital media, establishing a unified healthcare experience at scale.

Created and enforced platform-wide design standards and system governance, enabling consistency, speed, and quality across product, engineering, and editorial teams.

Aligned content, marketing, advertising, and media systems into a cohesive platform while ensuring usability, trust, and compliance within a highly regulated healthcare environment.

Outcomes

Key Results and Achievements

Successfully deployed healthcare platforms serving over 100 million users annually. Achieved 99.9% uptime for critical medical systems.

Implemented HIPAA-compliant data security measures protecting sensitive patient information. Established scalable SaaS infrastructure supporting thousands of healthcare organizations.

Developed comprehensive medical data management systems ensuring data integrity and regulatory compliance.

Created secure patient portals enabling seamless access to medical information.

Built integrated tools for medical professionals improving diagnostic accuracy and treatment outcomes.

Delivered a modernized, scalable health platform that improved usability, content accessibility, and user trust across high-traffic consumer experiences.

Established a unified design system and operational framework that accelerated in-house execution and reduced dependency on external agencies.

Enhanced platform performance, content discoverability, and monetization through integrated advertising, video, and marketing systems.

Created a durable foundation for continued growth, enabling WebMD to operate as a cohesive, design-led healthcare platform across digital channels.
