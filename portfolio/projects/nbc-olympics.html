Overview

Olympic Broadcasting Platform Development Outcomes

Architected comprehensive Olympic broadcasting platform supporting global live streaming and multi-platform content delivery.

Developed scalable infrastructure handling peak loads of hundreds of millions of viewers.

Implemented advanced audience analytics and engagement features for enhanced viewer experience.

The platform enables seamless distribution of Olympic content across traditional and digital channels, with real-time personalization and interactive features.

Advanced CDN and streaming technologies ensure high-quality delivery to global audiences with minimal latency.

Led experience strategy, UX/UI design, and live platform delivery for Xfinity’s Olympic and connected-TV initiatives, operating within a high-scale, real-time media environment.

Designed and executed immersive viewing experiences including live scores, medal counts, schedules, and editorial collections, while managing large volumes of dynamic content under live broadcast conditions.

Oversaw product integration of a voice-activated remote, enabling Olympic-specific voice commands within the Xfinity platform to enhance navigation and real-time content access.

Worked directly with editorial, engineering, and media operations teams to align platform design, content workflows, and performance requirements, ensuring accuracy, discoverability, and seamless delivery across connected TV experiences.
