Educational Technology Platform Development Outcomes

The platform provides educators with powerful tools for curriculum design, student assessment, and data-driven decision making.

Advanced analytics enable early intervention strategies and personalized learning experiences for improved educational outcomes.

Served as Design Director leading product, platform, and cross-functional delivery for statewide education and public-sector systems focused on real-time analytics, scientific modeling, and institutional decision-making.

Directed multiple teams across PHP, .NET, data science, QA, sales, and state-level leadership, aligning technical execution with policy, education standards, and operational needs.

Designed and guided platforms that translated complex mathematical and statistical models into usable systems for educators, administrators, and government stakeholders.
